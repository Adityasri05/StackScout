# StatSentry - Incident Management & Monitoring

High fidelity monitoring for business critical systems. Prevent downtime before it affects customers.

## Pricing Plans

### Entry Plan
- Price: $49 / month
- Features:
  - 50 HTTP/HTTPS checks
  - 1-minute frequency
  - Email & Slack notifications
  - Standard US hosting

### Growth Plan
- Price: $120 / month
- Features:
  - 250 checks
  - 30-second frequency
  - SMS & Webhook alerting
  - Slack & PagerDuty integrations
  - Custom headers & SSL check (citation: https://statsentry.net/pricing)
  - GDPR compliance with EU hosting in Frankfurt, Germany

### Enterprise Plan
- Price: $450 / month
- Features:
  - Unlimited checks
  - 10-second frequency
  - BAA for HIPAA compliance (citation: https://statsentry.net/security)
  - SOC 2 Type II assurance
  - 99.99% SLA with financial credits
  - Dedicated support manager

## Compliance
- SOC 2: Type II certified (citation: https://statsentry.net/compliance)
- GDPR: Fully Compliant. Data hosted in Frankfurt (DE).
- HIPAA: BAA supported on Enterprise Tier only (citation: https://statsentry.net/security)
