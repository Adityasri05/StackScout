# UptimeRadar - Pricing & Features

Simple and transparent pricing for developer teams.

## Plans

### Developer Tier
- Price: $12 / month (or $10 billed annually)
- Monitors: Up to 15 HTTP/HTTPS endpoints
- Frequency: 1 minute intervals
- Features:
  - Email alerts
  - Slack integration (citation: https://uptimeradar.io/pricing)
  - 1 Public Status Page
- Data Residency: US East (N. Virginia)

### Startup Tier
- Price: $39 / month
- Monitors: Up to 50 HTTP/HTTPS/SSL/TCP endpoints
- Frequency: 30 second intervals
- Features:
  - All Developer Features
  - SMS & Slack notifications
  - Webhooks
  - Multi-user access (up to 5 team members)
  - 3 Public Status Pages
  - EU Data Residency Option (citation: https://uptimeradar.io/pricing#features)

### Enterprise Tier
- Price: $99 / month
- Monitors: Unlimited endpoints
- Frequency: 10 second intervals
- Features:
  - Everything in Startup
  - Custom SLAs (99.9% uptime SLA)
  - SSO/SAML integration
  - Dedicated support channels

## Compliance & Security
- GDPR: Fully Compliant. We host European client data in Frankfurt, Germany.
- SOC 2: Undergoing Type I audit (expected Q4 2026).
- HIPAA: Not supported.

## Support
- Email support: 24/7 for all plans.
- Slack Support: Available on Startup and Enterprise plans. Response time guaranteed under 4 hours.
